## Reward Rules

Equipment gacha draws only equipment (no consumables or blueprints). Prices: 150G for 1, 750G for 6, 1400G for 13. Existing rarity rates are retained. When at least one limited item fits the player's per-item equipment cost cap for the rolled rarity, 15% of rolls select uniformly from eligible limited items; otherwise draw affordable normal equipment. Limited items are excluded from shops and adventure drops. Base cost is 3 for all three limited items. Existing ownership and saves are preserved.

All four new backgrounds join the duplicate-free 500G/one-ticket background skin pool as unowned prizes. Character result artwork has no background plate; background prizes display the awarded background.
