# Character Art Rules

- For new character skins and pose variants, follow `../../CHARACTER_STYLE_GUIDE.md` (user decision, 2026-11-10): non-pixel hand-drawn storybook art based on the user's own originals, warm brown pencil contours, expressive simple faces, broad softly textured color and restrained hatching. Reference the original artwork directly; generated samples are secondary.
- Use a centered, full-body character on a transparent square PNG canvas. Keep the complete pose and any held object inside the canvas with clear margins and consistent scale.
- Preserve each character concept across variants while making poses, gestures, hair, and silhouettes distinct. When a skin concept is offered as a reward, provide both male and female variants with the same theme.
- This is a children’s learning game. Use practical, fully covering adventurer clothing; do not generate swimsuits or revealing/sexualized designs.
- Remove the background completely and verify actual alpha transparency before adding the asset. Avoid glow backdrops, floor shadows, frames, text, and extra characters.
